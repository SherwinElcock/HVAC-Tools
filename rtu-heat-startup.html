RTU Startup Sequence — Heat Mode

Gas/Electric Rooftop Unit · Sequence of Operation & Startup Verification

Before energizing: Confirm gas valve is off during electrical checks, disconnect is lockable, and no open flame/spark sources near unit if checking for gas leaks. Follow lockout/tagout on 3-phase disconnects.

1. Pre-Start Inspection (Power OFF)

1
Visual inspection of unit
Check cabinet panels are secure, no visible corrosion/damage on heat exchanger access panel, curb flashing intact.
2
Verify line voltage at disconnect
Confirm voltage matches nameplate (±10%). Check all 3 legs on 3-phase for balance.
3
Check control transformer
Verify 24V secondary present, no blown fuse on board.
4
Inspect gas piping and connections
Check union, drip leg, manual shutoff valve position. Leak-check joints with soap solution or combustible gas detector before firing.
5
Confirm gas pressure at meter/regulator
Typical incoming: 7" W.C. natural gas (verify against unit's rated inlet pressure on nameplate).
6
Check filters, blower wheel, and coil
Replace/clean filters. Confirm blower wheel is clean and set screws are tight. Inspect evaporator coil for debris.
7
Verify venting / combustion air path
Confirm flue is unobstructed, draft inducer housing is sealed, and outdoor combustion air intake screens are clear.

2. Sequence of Operation — Call for Heat

1
Thermostat calls for heat (W1)
24V signal sent from thermostat to W terminal on control board.
2
Board runs safety circuit check
Control board verifies high-limit switch, rollout switches, and pressure switch are in normal (closed for high-limit/rollout, or appropriate state for pressure switch) position before proceeding.
3
Inducer motor (draft blower) energizes
Pre-purge begins — typically 15–30 seconds to clear combustion chamber of residual gas before ignition.
4
Pressure switch proves inducer airflow
Switch must close (prove negative pressure/draft) or ignition sequence halts and locks out — common fault point, check for blocked venting or weak inducer motor if this fails.
5
Ignition sequence begins
Hot Surface Igniter (HSI): Glows for 15–30 sec warm-up.
Intermittent Pilot (IPI/Spark): Spark generator clicks to ignite pilot, pilot proven by flame sensor before main gas valve opens.
6
Gas valve opens (main or combined pilot/main)
Gas flows to burners, ignites off HSI or proven pilot. Trial for ignition typically 4–7 seconds — if not proven, board retries limited number of times then locks out.
7
Flame sensor proves flame (rectification)
Microamp signal confirms stable flame — typically need >1–2 µA depending on manufacturer. If signal is lost, gas valve closes within 0.8 seconds (safety shutoff).
8
Heat exchanger warms — blower delay
Supply air blower stays off for a fan-on delay (typically 30–45 sec) to allow heat exchanger to warm before pushing air, preventing cold-air blow.
9
Indoor/supply blower energizes on heat speed
Blower ramps to heating CFM/tap — normally lower CFM than cooling to control temperature rise across heat exchanger.
10
Unit runs, maintaining setpoint
Monitor for consistent flame, stable gas pressure, and burner flame color/pattern.

3. Call for Heat Satisfied / Shutdown Sequence

1
Thermostat satisfied, W signal drops
Gas valve closes immediately, flame extinguishes.
2
Blower continues — off delay
Supply blower runs an additional 90–180 sec (varies by board) to extract remaining heat from exchanger, improving efficiency and preventing exchanger stress.
3
Inducer motor post-purge (if equipped)
Some units run inducer briefly after flame-out to clear flue gases.
4
System returns to standby
Ready for next call for heat or cool.

4. Startup Verification Checklist

Manifold gas pressure set correctly (compare to nameplate — typically 3.5" W.C. NG / 10" W.C. LP)
Temperature rise across heat exchanger within nameplate range (measure supply vs. return air temp)
Flame sensor microamp reading in acceptable range
No visible flame rollout or delayed ignition ("whump")
Burner flame is stable blue with defined inner cones — no yellow tipping/floating (incomplete combustion)
CO reading checked at flue with combustion analyzer (if available)
Blower amp draw within nameplate FLA
High-limit switch and rollout switches cycle test (if performing full commissioning)
Thermostat cycles unit properly through 2-3 calls for heat
Panels reinstalled, disconnect door closed, area clear

5. Common Fault Points

SymptomLikely Cause
Inducer runs, no ignitionBad igniter, gas valve not opening, flame sensor dirty/corroded
Ignition trial, flame drops outFlame sensor not grounded properly, weak microamp signal, gas pressure too low
Inducer won't start / pressure switch won't closeBlocked vent, bad inducer motor, cracked pressure switch hose, condensate blocking vent line
Short cycling on high limitAirflow restriction (dirty filter/coil), blower speed too low, blocked ductwork
Delayed ignition ("boom" on startup)Gas accumulation before ignition — check igniter output, gas valve response, or pre-purge timing
Nuisance lockoutsWeak flame signal, loose flame sensor wire, control board fault
Note: Sequence timing and terminal designations vary by manufacturer (Carrier, Trane, Lennox, York, etc.) — always cross-reference the specific unit's wiring diagram and IOM before troubleshooting a no-heat call.
