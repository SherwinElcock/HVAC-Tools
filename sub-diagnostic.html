High Superheat suction line

Likely Causes

  • Low refrigerant charge / undercharge
  • Refrigerant leak
  • Restriction in the liquid line, filter drier, or metering device starving the evaporator
  • Excess evaporator airflow or high heat load relative to charge
  • Metering device undersized or partially clogged

Resolution

  • Leak search the system; repair any leaks found
  • Recover and recharge to manufacturer's target superheat/subcooling
  • Replace filter drier and clear any line restriction
  • Confirm evaporator airflow and load are within design spec
  • Verify metering device is correctly sized and functioning
Low Superheat suction line

Likely Causes

  • Overcharged system
  • Low evaporator airflow — dirty filter/coil, weak blower, blocked ductwork
  • TXV stuck open or overfeeding
  • Oversized fixed orifice / metering device
  • Risk of evaporator flooding and liquid returning to the compressor

Resolution

  • Recover excess refrigerant to correct the charge
  • Clean coil and filter; verify blower speed and duct airflow
  • Inspect and replace a faulty TXV
  • Confirm correct metering device size for the system
High Subcooling liquid line

Likely Causes

  • Overcharged system
  • Restriction downstream of the condenser (kinked line, clogged filter drier) backing liquid up in the condenser
  • Non-condensables (air/moisture) in the system

Resolution

  • Recover refrigerant to bring charge back to spec
  • Locate and clear the restriction; replace the filter drier
  • Evacuate the system properly and recharge to remove non-condensables
Low Subcooling liquid line

Likely Causes

  • Undercharged system
  • Refrigerant leak
  • Insufficient condenser capacity — dirty coil, restricted airflow, weak condenser fan
  • High ambient temperature limiting condensing

Resolution

  • Leak search the system; repair any leaks found
  • Recharge to manufacturer's target subcooling
  • Clean the condenser coil and confirm proper airflow clearance
  • Verify condenser fan motor operation
